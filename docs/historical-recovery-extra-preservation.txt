Historical purchased-Extra membership during a missing recovery
==============================================================

Scope

Only historical_missing_recovery.v1 uses this refinement. No normal Extras,
Guest Commitments, handoff, staging, recovery, or import semantics change. No
schema or release metadata change. No commercial writer is added or invoked by
the classifier. This document and all fixtures use synthetic identities/times.

Previous behavior

HistoricalMissingRecoveryPreservation::compare independently compares complete
canonical history and current/future projections. inactiveTargetPreparation
allows a closed prior return checklist's next-pickup preparation membership to
retire when the recovery closes its target. It previously accepted only derived
cleaning/energy and already-inactive Extras verification. It rejected every
extra_fulfillment requirement, including a persisted pending fulfillment whose
action/relevance were already retired by an existing handoff. This made a valid
physical closure fail with unrelated current/future readiness membership changed.

Exception proof

The existing physical and verification branches keep their eligibility rules.
The separate recovery-only purchased branch requires all of the following:

* Retiring membership is exactly the canonical full-history next-preparation list; duplicates or unexplained members reject.
* Exact extra_fulfillment source and code, positive matching selection/fulfillment
  IDs, next_pickup_preparation phase, no commitment or synchronization requirement.
* Company, vehicle, source checklist, source trip, next target and purchased trip
  agree across canonical context, full projection and captured commercial inputs.
* Canonically closed source; target open before and canonically closed only by
  the proposed recovery. Closure uses actual lifecycle facts and allowed imported
  status, not calendar age. Only current/future membership disappears.
* Unsatisfied historical requirement already has action=null, actionable=false,
  relevant=false and retired_reason=target_handoff. Pending underlying fulfillment
  is incomplete and suppressed after handoff, with no completion timestamp/user/
  basis, no available actions, no blocker and no current synchronization work.
* No required/advisory/optional active refresh. No actionable/blocking purchased
  row or active manual row on the target. Uncertain or unsupported cases reject.
* Exact same requirement occurs once in full history before AND after, including
  unresolved pending status and review_required=true. Same purchased row remains
  visible in the commercial projection. Captured selection, fulfillment and
  snapshot ownership/identity are independently established. No source evidence
  or completion record changes.
* Independent current/future commercial projections and future schedule remain
  exactly equal. Existing custody, position, availability, readiness, movement
  execution and physical-work comparisons still run. Blocker aggregates must be
  derived from the canonical requirements; a changed count cannot hide an Extra.

A completed source does not make its target's real obligation historical. Actual
current/actionable or pre-refresh cases reject. The separate handoff preservation
policy is unchanged. Existing recovery-specific cleaning/energy and legitimate
physical-access deferral/release behavior remain in place; commercial obligations
are still visible even when physical work is deferred by guest possession.

Fingerprint and locking

The new expected_changes entry identifies source checklist/trip, target trip,
selection, fulfillment and pending completion state. Its evidence fingerprint
binds captured selection/completion data, snapshot, exact full historical rows,
purchased review visibility, source context and before/after lifecycle evidence.
A commercial fingerprint binds captured commercial inputs and both independently
compared commercial projections. Existing confirmation hashing also binds the
normalized request, actor, full scoped state, full captured inputs, canonical
before/after and streaming all-table preservation firewall.

Apply recomputes everything after the existing receipt, company/vehicle, trip,
commercial range and authorization locks. Changed identity, source payload,
completion, manual work or lifecycle makes the old confirmation stale. Existing
read-only preview and audited apply write sets are unchanged: recovery event,
return assessment and three operational audits; no existing row is edited.
Classification adds ZERO database queries. It scans only already captured,
bounded inputs, with existing inspection limits and fail-closed overflow.

Existing correct Trip A handoff and an older closed return are seeded, with an
unresolved inactive historical purchased Extra on A. Trips B/C initially have no
movement facts. A real future purchased Extra and manual task are also seeded.
All dates below are synthetic local application time, independent of any live
correction. C is the final physical holder despite incomplete imported history.

2     stageForChecklist B                   2023-06-10 10:20     operator / staged B
3     confirmGuestPickup B                  2023-06-10 13:15     guest / B
4     stageGuestReturn B                    2023-06-11 14:30     guest / B awaiting recovery

Persisted checklist/history/status rows remain byte-for-byte unchanged. The
fixture has no active positioning plans or airport workflow rows requiring
normal workflow synchronization; live preflight must inspect these separately.

Atomicity decision: A, conditional on a proven external observation fence

These workflows each own/commit their transaction. The privileged repair rejects
an outer transaction; pretending all seven calls are one database transaction
would require new orchestration and different semantics. The existing services
write local transactional facts/assessments and, where present, synchronously
update owned airport workflow history or invalidate positioning plans. The traced
paths have no external dispatch/outbox side effect. Current custody/readiness
are recomputed from committed facts. Separate commits are adequate INSIDE a
verified window in which no operator, HTTP/public reader, importer, cron, worker,
in-flight request or other database client can observe/use intermediate states.
The local synthetic harness has no such observers. It does not prove any actual
server is suspended, and no production connection was made to establish that.

A write-only maintenance screen is insufficient: public/read routes and direct
clients must be fenced too. Record each entry point, prior running state and
proof that it is stopped/blocked and drained. Retain the one controlled correction
session and necessary independent read-only verification. If the operator cannot
prove this fence, STOP before the first fact. Reassess orchestration; this change
does not silently substitute an atomic batch. No orchestration was implemented.

Mandatory gates, at entry and after EACH individual commit

1. Fresh protected backup before correction, with hash/access controls and restore
   evidence; establish and verify the complete observation/write/cron fence.
2. Resolve owned vehicle, trips, checklists, original handoff/assessment, statuses,
   full movement timeline and equivalent facts. Reconcile current reality anew;
   a later new movement or changed commercial input invalidates the old plan.
3. For step 1, obtain a new safe recovery preview and review its bound historical
   retirement, complete projections and exact confirmation. Apply that exact hash
   with one durable operation UUID. A non-safe preview blocks the sequence.
4. Before every next fact, re-read and verify the precise preceding event ID,
   code, owned trip, timestamp, location, source and assessment. Compare the full
   timeline against the recorded prefix; reject an unexpected concurrent event.
5. Check custody/basis and position against the table above, including the staged
   target association and unverified guest-return report. Validate strictly ordered
   actual handoff/recovery intervals and no overlap or duplicate lifecycle fact.
6. Compare unrelated commercial, checklist, schedule, availability, Damage and
   financial rows against the protected baseline. Ordinary target-owned workflow
   synchronization/audit must be identified and reviewed in advance. Unexpected
   invalidation of unrelated plans/authority is a blocker, never accepted silently.
7. Save exact event/assessment/audit receipts and committed state after each gate.
   Before the final handoff verify C's clean/known-charge stage and its location.
8. Reopen HTTP/cron/observers ONLY after all seven gates pass and final canonical
   custody, position, readiness, future obligations and business firewall agree.
   Restore only the processes/entry points that were running before the window.

Failure after a committed prefix

Failure after steps 1/2/3/4/5/6/7 leaves respectively: operator/no trip,
operator/staged B, guest/B, guest/B awaiting recovery, operator/no trip,
operator/staged C, guest/C (final verification/reopening still outstanding).
Keep all observers and writers suspended, stop and record the exact committed
prefix, identifiers, receipts, custody and position. No blind rollback of true
historical facts, no automatic re-run and no fresh UUID retry. Diagnose read-only
and explicitly review a resumed plan. A lost acknowledgement is not proof of
rollback: inspect/replay the original privileged UUID and inspect ordinary facts
for exact equivalents before any further action. If final verification fails,
even after step 7, the window stays closed.

Permanent validation

HistoricalMissingRecoveryExtraPreservationTest covers safe pending history,
preview purity, exact writes/replay, zero classification queries, ownership/action/
completion/source/history predicates, current/future commercial hard gates and
stale confirmations after commercial changes. HistoricalRecoverySequenceTest
executes all seven ordinary facts and independently interrupts after every commit,
checking exact prefixes, non-overlap, current position, unknown evidence separation,
future obligations, all unrelated tables and persisted checklist history.

The existing independent-process MariaDB harness adds committed commercial winners
(actionable configuration, valid completion, pending-row edit, source snapshot)
against a waiting recovery apply: all old fingerprints reject with zero repair
writes. A genuine ordinary catalog update also waits for a winning recovery and
its preservation firewall, then commits independently. No required skip is used.
The full local suite includes historical recovery/handoff, ordinary movement,
Extras/Commitments, Damage, wheel/repair, expenses, financial results and
reconciliation regressions. Test/build evidence is recorded in the ignored local
build directory; release metadata remains at the original baseline.
